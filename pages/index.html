TaxonMech

Microbial taxa and the strains behind them — NCBI Taxonomy identity, GTDB genomes, LPSN nomenclature and BacDive culture-collection strains, harmonized into one record per taxon from kg-microbe's transforms.

Source records live on the taxon

Every strain row carries its own genome and related records inline: NCBI, GTDB, BV-BRC/PATRIC, IMG and AllTheBacteria assemblies, and StrainInfo strain and deposit records with their DOIs and evidence. There is no separate per-source browser — open the taxon that classifies the strain.

Source, schema & bulk downloads

Records are LinkML-validated YAML under data/taxa/, seeded from committed inventories of kg-microbe's transforms. Download the complete harmonized taxon index (index.json.gz) or inspect the LinkML schema.
