The record is complete for the present BacDive-anchored strain model, but it
misses captured same-taxon GOLD and StrainInfo genome/culture evidence:

- `data/gold/organisms.jsonl.gz` contains exact same-taxon organisms
  `Go0123836`, `Go0384142`, and `Go0384250` for the base species, PT-14, and

- `data/gold/projects.jsonl.gz` links those three organisms to `Gp0132042`,
  `Gp0235231`, and `Gp0235341`, each with a BioProject and BioSample.
- `data/gold/analyses.jsonl.gz` links the three projects to analyses
  `Ga0113710`, `Ga0213144`, and `Ga0212615`, with GenBank assemblies
  `GCA_000826285.1`, `GCA_002179805.1`, and `GCA_002105255.1`.
- `data/straininfo/records.jsonl.gz` contains thirteen direct
  `NCBITaxon:29196` strain records, each carrying one same-taxon ATCC deposit.
- `data/straininfo/exclusions.tsv` records `no_eligible_own_deposit_match` for
  all thirteen same-taxon StrainInfo records.

This is a bounded gap in the source-link boundary, not YAML drift. GOLD
matches source deposits against `index_culture_identifiers` over the existing
BacDive-derived local strain inventory. StrainInfo similarly promotes only a
non-erroneous source deposit matched to an existing complete local culture
identifier. When a taxon has GOLD or StrainInfo culture deposits but no
BacDive strain row, the source evidence is captured and counted or excluded
but cannot be published in `strains[]`, genome records, or `related_records`.
