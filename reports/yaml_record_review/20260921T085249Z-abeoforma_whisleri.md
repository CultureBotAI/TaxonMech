  `Abeoforma whisleri ATCC PRA-280`, with GenBank assembly

This is a bounded gap in the source-link boundary, not YAML drift. GOLD
matches source deposits against `index_culture_identifiers` over the existing
BacDive-derived local strain inventory. When a taxon has GOLD culture deposits
but no BacDive strain row, the GOLD source evidence is captured and counted
but cannot be published in `strains[]`, `genome_assemblies`, or
`related_records`.
