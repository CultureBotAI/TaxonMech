- `data/straininfo/records.jsonl.gz` has no exact `NCBITaxon:1257118` source

  for `Acanthamoeba castellanii` ATCC 30010 / Neff under `NCBITaxon:5755`, and
  that row is excluded from the StrainInfo projection with
  `no_eligible_own_deposit_match`.

This is a bounded gap in the source-link boundary, not YAML drift. GOLD
matches source deposits against `index_culture_identifiers` over the existing
BacDive-derived local strain inventory. StrainInfo similarly promotes only a
non-erroneous source deposit matched to an existing complete local culture
identifier. When a taxon has GOLD culture deposits, or same-deposit StrainInfo
records at a parent rank, but no BacDive strain row, the source evidence is
captured and counted or excluded but cannot be published in `strains[]`,
genome records, or `related_records`.
