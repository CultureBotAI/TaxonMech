This is a bounded gap in the source-link boundary, not YAML drift. GOLD and
StrainInfo linkers both match source deposits against `index_culture_identifiers`
over the existing BacDive-derived local strain inventory. When a taxon has
NCBI/GOLD/StrainInfo culture deposits but no BacDive strain row, the source
evidence is captured and counted or excluded but cannot be published in
`strains[]` or `related_records`.
