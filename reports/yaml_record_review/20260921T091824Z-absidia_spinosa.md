This is a bounded gap in the source-link boundary, not YAML drift. GOLD
matches source deposits against `index_culture_identifiers` over the existing
BacDive-derived local strain inventory. StrainInfo similarly promotes only a
non-erroneous source deposit matched to an existing complete local culture
identifier. When a taxon has GOLD or StrainInfo culture deposits but no
BacDive strain row, the source evidence is captured and counted or excluded
but cannot be published in `strains[]` or `related_records`.
